Trigonometric ratios

In a right triangle, each ratio compares two sides relative to the angle θ.

sin θ = opposite / hypotenuse

cos θ = adjacent / hypotenuse

tan θ = opposite / adjacent

The basic identity

This holds for every angle and is the base of most proofs in the chapter.

sin²θ + cos²θ = 1
